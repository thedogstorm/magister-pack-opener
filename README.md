# 🎁 Magister Pack Opener

**Turn your Magister grades into packs and discover your collection!**

Magister Pack Opener is a browser extension that transforms your Magister grades into virtual packs. Open packs, discover different rarities, build your collection and optionally synchronize your progress with your online account.

🌐 **Website:** https://stormproductionsgroup.base44.app/

---

## ✨ Features

- 🎁 **Virtual Packs** – Open packs generated using information from your Magister account.
- ⭐ **Multiple Rarities** – Discover items ranging from Common to Perfect.
- 📊 **Grade Integration** – Use your Magister grades as the basis for your packs.
- 📚 **Grade Details** – Optionally include subjects, grades, descriptions and weighting factors.
- ☁️ **Account Synchronization** – Connect your extension to the website to save your pack-opening progress.
- 📈 **Pack Statistics** – Keep track of your opened packs and discovered rarities.

## 🔗 Connect Your Account

You can connect Magister Pack Opener to your online account.

1. Visit the [Magister Pack Opener website](https://stormproductionsgroup.base44.app/).
2. Log in using your email or Google account.
3. Copy your account-linking code.
4. Open the extension and paste your code into the account connection section.
5. Optionally enable detailed grade synchronization.

When connected, your pack-opening activity can be synchronized with your account.

## 🔒 Privacy

Your privacy matters.

- Account synchronization is optional.
- Grade details are only synchronized when you enable the corresponding option.
- A derived identifier is used instead of your original Magister ID when synchronizing pack information.
- Your Magister authentication token is used to retrieve information from Magister and is not intended to be shared with the website.

Read the full [Privacy Policy](https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/magister-pack-opener-privacy/).

## 🌐 Browser Compatibility

Magister Pack Opener is developed as a browser extension for Chromium-based browsers.

Availability depends on the browser and its extension store.
