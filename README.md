# 🎁 Magister Pack Opener

**Turn your Magister grades into packs and discover your collection!**

Magister Pack Opener is a browser extension that turns every new grade in Magister into a virtual pack. Your grades stay hidden until you open the pack. Open packs, discover rarities from Common to Perfect, build your collection, and optionally sync your progress with your online account.

🌐 **Website:** https://stormproductionsgroup.base44.app/
🧩 **Get the extension:** [Microsoft Edge Add-ons](https://microsoftedge.microsoft.com/addons/detail/magister-pack-opener/kiekgjjnhnpogmnlloddlbfepbjnenno)

> The extension and website are in Dutch.

## ✨ Features

- 🎁 **Virtual Packs** – Every new grade in Magister becomes a pack with an opening animation.
- 🙈 **Spoiler-free** – Grades stay hidden until you open their pack, including on the start page.
- ⭐ **Six Rarities** – Common, Rare, Legendary, Mythic, Icon and Perfect, based on the grade (Perfect is a 10).
- 📊 **Pack Statistics** – Keep track of your opened packs and discovered rarities, with a back-up and restore code.
- 🧮 **Grade Calculator** – A small calculator button in the bottom-right corner of Magister. Click it, then click grades from your Magister list to add them, and instantly see your weighted and unweighted average, averages per subject, and which grade you still need for a target of your choice. Grades still in an unopened pack only appear after you open the pack.
- ☁️ **Account Synchronization** – Connect the extension to the website to save your opened packs and view your collection there.
- 📚 **Grade Details** – Optionally include subject, grade, description and weighting for each pack, also for packs you opened earlier (as long as the grade is still in your latest 300 grades in Magister).
- ♻️ **Restore from Account** – Reinstalled the extension or switched computers? Restore your opened packs and statistics from your account.
- 🛠️ **Control Panel** – Version history, an overview of the rarity levels, help, account settings and a check for updates.

## 🔗 Connect Your Account

1. Visit the [Magister Pack Opener website](https://stormproductionsgroup.base44.app/).
2. Log in using your email or Google account.
3. Copy your link code.
4. Open the extension's control panel and paste your code in the **Account** tab.
5. Optionally enable detailed grade synchronization (subject, grade, description and weighting).

Once connected, your opened packs are synchronized with your account. You can turn the details option off at any time, and disconnect whenever you like.

## 🔒 Privacy

Your privacy matters.

- Account synchronization is **optional**. Nothing is sent until you connect.
- Grade details are only synchronized when you enable the corresponding option, and only for packs you have opened yourself. Grades in unopened packs are never sent.
- A derived identifier is used instead of your original Magister ID.
- The extension does not store your grades in your browser. It only keeps which packs are opened, their rarity, and your settings.
- The grade calculator works locally. Nothing from it is stored or sent.
- Your Magister authentication token is only used to retrieve your grades from Magister and is not sent to the website.

Read the full [Privacy Policy](https://stormproductionsgroup.base44.app/privacy).

## 🌐 Browser Compatibility

Magister Pack Opener is built for Chromium-based browsers (Manifest V3) and is available on Microsoft Edge Add-ons. Availability in other stores depends on the browser.
