# Privacy Policy – Magister Pack Opener

**Last updated:** November 6, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Magister Pack Opener ("the Extension") is a browser extension that allows users to open virtual packs using information from their Magister account.

Your privacy is important. This policy explains what information the Extension accesses, how it is used, where it is stored, and whether it is shared.

## 2. Information We Access

The Extension may access the following information:

- **Personal information:** Magister user identifiers and student-related information.
- **Authentication information:** Authentication tokens used to communicate with the Magister API.
- **Website content:** Grades, subjects, descriptions, weighting factors and other relevant Magister data.
- **User activity:** Pack-opening activity, timestamps and virtual item rarity information.

The Extension does not intentionally access health information, financial information, payment information, personal communications, geographical location or general browsing history.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

Information accessed by the Extension is used exclusively to:

- Retrieve relevant information from Magister.
- Calculate and display virtual pack contents.
- Display grades and related information where necessary.
- Save pack-opening statistics and user preferences.
- Provide optional account synchronization features, if enabled.

## 4. Data Storage

The Extension uses browser storage to save relevant settings and statistics locally.

Magister authentication tokens are used to authenticate requests to Magister.

Optional account synchronization features may transmit relevant information to an external server if configured and enabled. These features are disabled by default in the current configuration.

## 5. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information.

Information is not shared with advertisers or data brokers.

The Extension communicates with Magister to retrieve information requested by the user.

If optional synchronization is enabled, relevant information may be transmitted to the configured synchronization server.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored Extension data remains in browser storage until deleted by the user or removed through browser data management.

Users can remove locally stored information by clearing the Extension's stored data or uninstalling the Extension.

Information transmitted through optional synchronization may remain on the configured server until deleted according to that server's data retention procedures.

## 7. Security

The Extension takes reasonable measures to protect information accessed and processed through its functionality.

Authentication tokens are used to communicate with Magister and should not be shared with unauthorized parties.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

## 8. Third-Party Services

The Extension interacts with Magister, an independent third-party educational platform.

The Extension is not affiliated with, endorsed by or officially associated with Magister unless explicitly stated otherwise.

Use of Magister is subject to its own privacy policy and terms.

## 9. Children's Privacy

The Extension may be used by students, including individuals under the age of 16.

The Extension does not intentionally collect additional personal information from children beyond the information necessary for its functionality.

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the Extension's functionality or data practices change.

The latest version will be made available at the publicly accessible privacy policy URL.
