# Privacy Policy – Magister Pack Opener

**Last updated: November 6, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

Magister Pack Opener is a browser extension that allows users to open virtual packs using information retrieved from their Magister account.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the extension accesses, how it is processed, and what happens when users connect their account to our website.

## 2. Information We Access

The extension may access:

- Magister user identifiers, used locally to distinguish users.
- Authentication tokens required to retrieve information from Magister.
- Grades, subjects, descriptions and weighting factors.
- Pack-opening activity, including pack rarity and opening timestamps.
- A derived identifier used to associate pack-opening records without transmitting the original Magister ID.

## 3. Local Processing

The extension accesses Magister information to generate virtual packs and calculate their contents.

Information required for the extension's functionality may be stored locally in browser storage.

Magister authentication tokens are used to authenticate requests to Magister. They are not intentionally transmitted to our website as part of account synchronization.

## 4. Optional Website Account Synchronization

Users can optionally connect their extension to our website:

https://stormproductionsgroup.base44.app/

To connect, users log in to the website, obtain an account-linking code and enter that code in the extension.

When connected, the extension sends:

- The account-linking code during the linking process.
- The rarity level of each opened pack (Common to Perfect).
- The date of each pack opening.
- A derived identifier instead of the original Magister ID.

Users can additionally enable the option:

"Also save subject, grade, description and weighting to my account."

When this option is enabled, the following additional information is synchronized:

- Subject
- Grade
- Description
- Weighting factor

Sharing these additional grade details is optional and requires the user to enable the checkbox.

No synchronization data is transmitted before an account is connected.

## 5. Disconnecting an Account

Users can disconnect their extension from their website account by generating a new account-linking code on the website.

The extension uses the new code for subsequent account linking.

Previously synchronized information may remain stored on the website and is subject to the website's data retention and deletion procedures.

## 6. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information.

Information synchronized through the optional account connection is transmitted to our website, Storm Productions Group.

We do not intentionally share this information with advertisers or data brokers.

The extension also communicates with Magister to retrieve the information needed for its functionality.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored information remains in browser storage until deleted by the user or removed through browser data management.

Information synchronized with the website may remain stored in the user's website account until deleted according to the website's data retention procedures.

Users who wish to request deletion of synchronized information can contact the website operator.

## 8. Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information processed by the extension and website.

Authentication tokens are used to communicate with Magister and are not intended to be shared with the website.

No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 9. Third-Party Services

The extension interacts with Magister, an independent educational platform.

Website account functionality is provided through:

https://stormproductionsgroup.base44.app/

These services may have their own privacy policies and terms of service.

Magister Pack Opener is not officially affiliated with or endorsed by Magister.

## 10. Children's Privacy

The extension may be used by students, including minors.

The extension processes student-related information only to provide its functionality and optional account synchronization.

Users should only connect accounts and synchronize information when authorized to do so.

## 11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the extension's functionality or data practices change.

The latest version will be published at the publicly accessible privacy policy URL.

## 12. Contact

For privacy-related questions or data deletion requests, please use the contact options available on the website.
